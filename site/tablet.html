Open The Designer

On A Tablet, With Or Without Signal

Stand in the customer's yard, lay out the building in front of them and hand over a price before you leave.

No Signal NeededOpen the designer once while you have a connection. After that it opens and works with none. Nothing about the job is sent anywhere.
The Job Stays On The TabletEvery change saves on the device as you make it. Close the tab, open it tomorrow, and the job is where you left it.
Take The Job Back To The OfficeSave a job to a file, send it by email or carry it on a thumb drive, and open it on the office computer. The file carries the building, the pricing and the price list in use.
Sized For ThumbsOn a touch screen the buttons and steppers grow. On a narrow screen the building takes the top and the settings and material list sit side by side below it.
Add It To The Home ScreenIt carries its own icon, so it can sit on the home screen next to your other apps.

Not In It Yet

  • A run on a real tablet. The layouts were checked at tablet sizes on a computer, not yet on the device itself. Adding it to a home screen has not been tried on one either.
  • Jobs that keep themselves in step between two devices. A job moves as a file you save and open, not on its own.
