Open The Designer

For Lumber Yards

Hand your builders a designer that adds up jobs at your prices and sends the material order back to you.

Your Price List, In Their HandsFill in one spreadsheet with four columns: Item, Size, Unit and Price. Save it as CSV and send it to your builders. They load it once and every job adds up at your prices.
A Price List For Each BuilderType a builder's name and a percent up or down, and the program saves a copy of your list at that builder's prices, ready to send.
An Order You Can Open In A SpreadsheetThe material order saves as a CSV file with one line per item: quantity, item, size, length, price each and cost.
Nothing To InstallIt opens in a browser. A builder with ten people or fewer pays nothing to use it.
Your Yard's LicenseA yard with ten people or fewer pays nothing. A larger yard buys a license. How the count works.

Not In It Yet

  • Your yard's logo on the designer. Your company name and contact details print on the paperwork.
  • Manufacturer catalogs built in. Every item comes from the price list you load.
  • Excel files. Price lists load as CSV.
  • Orders sent to you from inside the program. The builder saves the order file and sends it.
