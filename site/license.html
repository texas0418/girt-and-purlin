The License, In Plain Words

Small shops use it without paying. Larger companies buy a license. Anyone can read the code.

The First 30 DaysEvery company uses the whole program for 30 days. After that it asks for a license code, and your jobs stay on your device while you get one.
Ten People Or FewerYour code costs nothing. Write to simon@simonbuilds.app with your company name and how many people work for you. A code runs for one year and is renewed the same way.
More Than Ten PeopleBuilders and contractors pay $1,500 a year, or $149 a month. Lumber yards and suppliers pay $4,800 a year. The first ten paying companies keep the price they signed at. To buy, write to simon@simonbuilds.app.
Who CountsEmployees and regular subcontractors, across the whole company. A parent company and sister companies count together.
Source-AvailableThe code is public and you can read all of it. Because larger companies must pay, it is source-available and not open source.
