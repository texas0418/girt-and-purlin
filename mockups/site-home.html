What It Does

Catches A Post Under A DoorPut a walk door where a post lands and it tells you in plain words, then offers to slide the door clear.→ Prices From Your Own ListLoad a spreadsheet of your supplier's prices. Add your markup, labor and sales tax, and the total keeps up.→ Prints The PaperworkA proposal for the customer, a material order for the yard, a cut list for the crew and framing drawings of every wall.→ Sends The Customer A LinkThey open it on a phone and turn their own building around. Your costs and markup stay with you.→ Works On A Tablet Without SignalOpen it once and it keeps working in a field with no bars. The job saves on the tablet.→ Packs A Folder For Your EngineerIt does not do the engineering. It hands your engineer the size, the framing as drawn, every post and opening, and the drawings.→

No Charge For Shops Of Ten Or Fewer

Builders and yards with ten people or fewer use the whole program without paying. Larger companies buy a license. The code is public for anyone to read, which makes it source-available.

Where It Stands Today

Version 1 draws plain rectangular buildings with a gable roof. Its framing rules are still being checked against published builder guides, and the drawings say so until that is done.
