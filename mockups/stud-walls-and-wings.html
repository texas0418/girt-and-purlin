Stud Spacing

Stud walls stand on a concrete slab or foundation instead of posts set in the ground. Each wall gets a treated bottom plate, studs, two top plates and a header over every door and window. The trusses sit 2 ft apart on top of the walls.

The post choices (post spacing, post size, footings) are hidden while Stud Walls is picked. The concrete under the walls is your engineer's to design and is not counted here.

How A Stud Wall Is Drawn (Framing View And Wall Sheets)

Every opening gets a header, a full stud and a shorter stud under the header on each side, and short studs above. The material order and cut list count each piece. Wall sheathing and house wrap are added under steel panels on a stud wall.
